Email Finder

Feed it a name and a website — it goes through the homepage and contact pages looking for a visible email, then drafts a personalized pitch email for every lead it finds one for. No sign-up, no daily limit.

⚠ GEMINI_API_KEY isn't set on the server — emails are still being found, but personalized drafts won't generate until it's added.
⚠ Name-only entries need GOOGLE_CUSTOM_SEARCH_API_KEY and GOOGLE_CUSTOM_SEARCH_CX set on the server to search for businesses without a website.
Two formats, mix freely: Business Name, website.com for leads with a site — or just Business Name on its own line for leads with no website.
